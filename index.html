Game Description

Enemies

  • Chickens: Small, normal, and big chickens block your way.
  • End Boss: Stronger enemy at the end with 100 HP.

How to Attack

  • Jump Attack: Jump on any chicken from above to defeat it.
  • Bottle Attack: Throw bottles to kill all enemies, including the boss.

Boss Fight

Only bottles can damage the boss. Each bottle deals 20 HP damage — hit the boss 5 times to win.

Bottles & Coins

  • Start with 5 bottles.
  • Collect more bottles along the way by running over them.
  • Collect coins as you explore.
